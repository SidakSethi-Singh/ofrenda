# Afternoons at Chapultepec Park
**Told by:** Carlos (Brother)

Every spring on Saturday mornings, Rosa would pack a woven wicker basket with cold agua de jamaica and sliced jicama dusted with lime and chile. Her favorite place to walk was Chapultepec Park, beneath the towering ancient ahuehuete trees near the quiet botanical gardens.

She would sit on the curved stone bench in the shade, watching families fly bright tissue paper kites against the blue sky. She loved Chapultepec because the trees reminded her of the deep forests outside her childhood village in Oaxaca, and she would spend hours sketching birds and marigolds in the margins of her pocket book.
