# Sunday Mole and the Clay Pot
**Told by:** Mateo (Grandson)

Every Sunday afternoon, the entire courtyard would fill with the warm, smoky scent of toasted chiles and roasted almonds. Abuela Rosa always cooked her signature mole negro in a round earthenware clay pot that her mother had given her in 1968. She ground Mexican cinnamon bark, dried mulato chiles, and blocks of bitter dark chocolate by hand on her volcanic stone metate. 

She insisted that good food required quiet attention. Her favorite dish to serve was braised chicken drenched in that rich mole, topped with toasted sesame seeds and fresh handmade corn tortillas. Whenever someone asked for her secret recipe, she would smile, wipe flour from her apron, and say: "The only secret ingredient is thirty minutes of extra patience over the low flame."
