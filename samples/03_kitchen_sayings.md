# The Worn Notebook and Morning Sayings
**Told by:** Sofia (Granddaughter)

Abuela Rosa kept a small leather notebook wrapped in string on the kitchen counter, next to her jar of star anise. Whenever we hurried through breakfast or worried about school exams, she would lay her hand gently over ours and repeat her favorite saying: "Donde come uno, comen dos, y donde hay calma, florece la vida."

Her other constant proverb, painted directly on a ceramic tile above the hearth, was: "Patience in the pot, peace in the soul." She always advised us that rushing through life only spoils the broth, and that kindness given freely never leaves a house empty.
