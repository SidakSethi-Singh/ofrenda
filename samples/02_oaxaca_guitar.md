# The Oaxaca Guitar and Twilight Songs
**Told by:** Lucia (Daughter)

When the sunset faded into violet over the tiled roofs, Abuela Rosa would take down her cedar wood guitar from the wooden peg in the hallway. Her father had hand-carved that guitar for her in Oaxaca back in 1954 before she moved to the capital. The wood had turned honey-gold with decades of oil from her fingertips.

Her favorite song in the whole world was "Cielito Lindo", but whenever autumn rain tapped against the glass windows, she used to sing "La Llorona" in a tender, hushed voice that made the room grow still. She taught all of her children the fingerpicking pattern on that old cedar guitar, humming along with the kettle boiling on the stove.
